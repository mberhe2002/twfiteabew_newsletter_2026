From the field

A careful look at every Gubae Bet

In early September, Dn. Gebru, our full-time training coordinator in Mekelle, completed a review of every Gubae Bet that Twfite Abew supports. He observed classes in person and listened to teachers, student coordinators, clergy, and the faithful in Axum, Adigrat, Kuiha, Mai Anbesa, and Maichew. On September 10, our committee met with the Tigray team to go through his findings together.

What we are doing next:

  • Shared bylaws and a code of conduct for all Gubae Bets, so every school works from the same clear expectations.
  • A clear curriculum, with fair, transparent student selection and a consistent way to evaluate learning.
  • Training for teachers and student representatives, centered on the purpose of Twfite Abew.
  • Keeping our focus on scripture interpretation (Metsahift Tirguame), the heart of our mission to form Gospel teachers.
  • Strengthening Gubae Bets based in monasteries, where students can study with fewer distractions.

Stories of the season

Where your support went

Books delivered to the Axum Tsion Gubae Bet with the support of Twfite Abew

Axum Tsion

Teaching resumes and new books arrive

After the passing of our beloved Memhirt Hiwot, the Axum Tsion Gubae Bet paused for a time. Two teachers have now been assigned, and teaching has resumed as before. In her memory, Twfite Abew provided 50,000 Birr for books. In late October, 41 books covering 32 titles were delivered to the students.

Mai Anbesa Gedam

A harvest growing through drought

This season's rains were short, and the monastery's crops began to dry out. Twfite Abew covered the cost of a water pump so the fields could be irrigated. By October the crops had recovered, and both corn and teff were growing well.

Young corn on the terraces, late August

Teff growing well, October

The 16 students at Mai Anbesa, all selected through a competitive entrance exam, continue their studies faithfully. Arrangements have also been made to provide each of them with soap and other basic hygiene supplies.
